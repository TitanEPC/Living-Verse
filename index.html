Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

App Name: Living Verse Live Wall Paper

1. Introduction

Welcome to Living Verse Live Wall Paper. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data related to your device. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information when you download, install, and use the app.

2. Information Collection and Use

Our app is designed with your privacy as a fundamental priority. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information or user data.

  • No Personal Data: We do not track, collect, or store your name, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Local Image Processing: The app allows you to select and upload custom image backgrounds from your device to personalize your live wallpaper. These images are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded, shared, or sent to any external servers or third-party cloud storage.
  • App Functionality: The app dynamically displays scrolling Bible verses directly on your device screen. This feature operates entirely offline or locally without tracking your reading habits or preferences.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The app offers optional monetization choices, including a monthly subscription and a lifetime one-time payment option. All financial transactions, recurring subscription billing, and lifetime purchases are handled securely and directly through the Google Play Store.

  • We do not collect, process, or store your credit card information, banking details, or billing addresses.
  • Subscription statuses, renewals, cancellations, and purchase verifications are managed entirely by Google Play’s billing infrastructure in accordance with Google Play Policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Because the app does not collect analytics, track usage, or use third-party advertising networks, no user data is shared with outside entities, except for the standard billing verifications processed by Google Play.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13, nor does it contain any features directed at collecting personal information from minors.
